# Give A Wonderful Day — Social Media Content

> Crypto-forward. Direct-impact. Brand-aligned: donations in, wonderful days out.

## X / Twitter — Main Announcement Thread

**Post 1 (hook)**
> Give A Wonderful Day takes donations. It distributes funds to people who need a wonderful day — terminally ill patients, families facing life-limiting circumstances, children who deserve a birthday worth remembering.
>
> No middleman opacity. Donations go through our audited `NonprofitDonation.sol` smart contract. Every contribution produces a verifiable event. Funds are withdrawn through a multisig treasury for approved recipient programs.
>
> That is the brand. Simple.

**Post 2 (mission)**
> Our flagship program: the Wonderful Birthday Project. Three tiers — Wonderful, Extraordinary, Dream — designed around real family needs, not marketing levels. Two tracks: Priority / Emergency (rolling review) and Periodic Selection (transparent, equitable).
>
> No influence from wealth, social following, or promotion ability. Just verified need and a team that delivers.

**Post 3 (crypto + transparency)**
> Why crypto fits: conventional donation flows hide where money goes. A blockchain donation record does not. Every ETH donation to the contract produces:
> • A verifiable transaction hash
> • An emitted event
> • A public explorer link
> • Aggregate impact reporting — without exposing recipient PII

**Post 4 (call to action)**
> We are not asking for speculation. We are asking for support. Donate ETH directly. Verify the event. See the impact reported publicly. Help make a birthday wonderful for a child facing a severe medical circumstance.
> Contract and donation flow: docs/LAUNCH_PLAN.md and the repo.

**Post 5 (disclaimer / trust)**
> This is a direct-impact proposal, not an investment offer, not a token raise, not a claim that donations are tax-deductible unless independently verified. Legal, tax, and security approvals are prerequisites — not afterthoughts.
